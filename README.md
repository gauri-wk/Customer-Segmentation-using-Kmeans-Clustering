## Customer Segmentation using K-Means Clustering

## Project Overview

This project implements Customer Segmentation using the K-Means Clustering algorithm, an unsupervised machine learning technique. The objective is to group customers into distinct clusters based on their characteristics and purchasing behavior, enabling businesses to better understand their customer base and develop targeted marketing strategies.

## Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses often have large customer bases with varying purchasing patterns and preferences. Identifying meaningful customer segments helps organizations:

-Understand customer behavior
-Improve customer retention
-Design personalized marketing campaigns
-Optimize business strategies
-Increase overall customer satisfaction

## Results

The model successfully segmented customers into distinct groups based on patterns present in the dataset. These customer segments can be utilized for:

-Targeted marketing campaigns
-Customer relationship management
